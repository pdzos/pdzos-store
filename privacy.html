Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 2, 2026

1. Overview

PDzOS Store ("we", "us", or "our") is an independent, static Android software catalog. We believe in privacy by design and operational transparency. This privacy statement explains how information is handled when you browse this website and download applications.

2. No User Accounts or Personal Profiles

You can browse PDzOS Store and view all applications without creating an account, registering an email address, or submitting any identifying personal information. We do not maintain any user database or authentication system.

3. Local Browser Storage

This website utilizes local browser storage (HTML5 localStorage) strictly for client-side user preferences:

  • Theme preference (Dark mode or Light mode).
  • Local download counter for your current browser session.

This data remains entirely on your personal device and is never transmitted to our servers.

4. External Download Hosting Providers

Application binaries (APK files) are not hosted on this website's web server. When you download an app, your request connects directly to third-party public hosting providers, which may include:

  • Google Drive (Google LLC) — Subject to Google's Privacy Policy.
  • GitHub Releases (GitHub Inc. / Microsoft) — Subject to GitHub's Privacy Statement.

These external platforms may log standard technical connection metadata (such as IP addresses and user agents) in accordance with their respective privacy policies.

5. Analytics Configuration

By default, analytics tracking is disabled in our code base. If privacy-friendly server-level analytics (such as Cloudflare Web Analytics, which does not use cookies or collect personal identities) are enabled by the site operator, they are used solely to assess aggregate site traffic and bandwidth.

6. Contact

If you have questions or inquiries regarding this privacy statement, you can reach out through our official GitHub repository.
